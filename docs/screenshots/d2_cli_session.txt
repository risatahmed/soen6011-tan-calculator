tan(x) calculator (Deliverable 2 -- from-scratch core, Tkinter GUI also available via 'python3 -m src.gui')
  - Enter x as a real number.
  - Default angle unit is RADIANS; prefix with 'd:' to enter degrees (e.g. 'd:45').
  - Supported range: |x| <= 10000 radians (after unit conversion).
  - Values at or within tolerance of a pole (x = pi/2 + k*pi) are rejected
    with an explanation rather than returning a huge/meaningless number.
  - Results are displayed to 10 significant figures.
  - Type 'quit' or 'exit' to leave.
