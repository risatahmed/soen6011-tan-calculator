tan(x) calculator (Deliverable 1 prototype)
  - Enter x as a real number, in RADIANS (not degrees).
  - Supported range: |x| <= 10000 radians.
  - Values at or within tolerance of a pole (x = pi/2 + k*pi) are rejected
    with an explanation rather than returning a huge/meaningless number.
  - Results are displayed to 10 significant figures.
  - Type 'quit' or 'exit' to leave.
